Frequently asked

What ages is JUMVI for?
JUMVI is designed for ages 3–8. Missions are grouped into three age tracks (3–5, 6–8, 8+), so games always match your kid's stage. Adult supervision is recommended for the youngest players.
Do I need to download an app?
No. The QR Mission Card opens the JUMVI Mission Hub in any browser — no download, no account, no email needed to play.
Does it need batteries or a subscription?
No batteries, no subscription, ever. The set is ready to play out of the box, and all 36 missions are included.
Is it safety tested?
Yes. JUMVI is third-party lab tested to CPSIA and ASTM F963-23 toy safety standards, with rounded edges and kid-safe materials. Please note the small-ball choking warning: not for children under 3.
Can we play indoors?
Absolutely. The paddles are ultra-light EVA foam and several missions are designed specifically for living-room-friendly play.
Is it beach and pool friendly?
Yes. The fabric is splash-friendly and wipe-clean — rinse, dry and play again. The mesh bag lets wet gear breathe on the way home.
What if we lose a ball?
The set includes 4 balls, so there's always a spare in the mesh bag. If you ever run out, contact us at support@jumvi.co — we'll help make it right.
Where can I buy JUMVI?
JUMVI is sold on Amazon.com with fast Prime shipping and Amazon's standard 30-day return policy.
