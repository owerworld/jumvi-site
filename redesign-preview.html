FAQ

Good questions.

What ages is JUMVI for?
JUMVI is designed for ages 3–12. Younger kids can start with simpler catches and shorter distances, while older kids can use more advanced timed, movement and team challenges. Adult supervision is recommended for younger players.
Do I need to download an app?
No. The Mission Hub opens in a mobile browser. There is no app download and no signup required to play.
How many people can play?
The complete set is designed for 2–4 players, with four paddles and four sticky balls. Some missions can also be adapted for individual practice.
Does it need batteries or a subscription?
No. The physical set needs no batteries and the 36 guided missions are included without a subscription.
Is it safety tested?
JUMVI is third-party lab tested to CPSIA and ASTM F963-23 toy safety standards. Choking hazard: the set contains a small ball and is not for children under 3.
Can we use it indoors and outdoors?
Yes. JUMVI is designed for flexible active play in suitable indoor spaces as well as backyards, parks and other outdoor settings.
