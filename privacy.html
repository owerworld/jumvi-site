Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This site (jumvi.co) is operated by SAY23 LLC, 8 The Green, Suite 19606, Dover, DE 19901, USA. This policy explains what information we collect here, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: support@jumvi.co.

Short version: the only personal information we ask for on this site is an email address, and only if you choose to request the free Mission Book. We don't sell it, we don't run ad trackers, and you can have it deleted at any time by emailing us.

1. Information we collect

  • Email address — only if you submit it through the Mission Book form. Nothing else in that form is required.
  • Basic technical data — our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard request data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use this to build a profile of you.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use this page (which sections are read, where people scroll, which buttons are clicked). Clarity records anonymised session activity and sets a cookie for this purpose. It automatically masks text input, and we do not use it to identify individual visitors. You can opt out via Microsoft's opt-out page.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or retargeting pixels on this site.

2. How we use it

  • To send you the printable Mission Book you requested.
  • To occasionally let you know when new mission packs or products launch. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • To answer you if you contact our support address.

We do not sell, rent or trade your email address, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

3. Service providers

We use a small number of processors who may handle data on our behalf:

  • Vercel Inc. — website hosting and server logs.
  • Formspree Inc. — receives and forwards the Mission Book form submission to our support inbox.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this site.
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymous website usage analytics and heatmaps. See Microsoft's privacy statement.

Each of these processes data under its own privacy terms. We do not give them permission to use your information for their own marketing.

4. Children's privacy

JUMVI is a toy for children, but this website and the Mission Book signup are intended for parents and other adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, email support@jumvi.co and we will delete it promptly.

The JUMVI Mission Hub, which is unlocked by the QR card inside the product box, is designed so that children's progress stays on the device and no account or email is required to play.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR in the EU/UK and the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its use. Because the only thing we typically hold is an email address, most requests are quick to fulfil.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@jumvi.co. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

6. Retention

We keep email addresses until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Server logs are kept only for as long as our hosting provider retains them for operational and security purposes.

7. International transfers

SAY23 LLC is a US company and our providers are primarily US-based, so information you send us is processed in the United States.

8. Purchases on Amazon

Links on this site take you to Amazon.com to buy. Any order, payment, shipping and account information is collected and handled by Amazon under Amazon's own privacy notice, not ours. We never see your payment details.

9. Changes

If we update this policy we will change the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be noted on our homepage.
